About Me

Designing, coding & capturing moments.

I move between disciplines on purpose — a UI screen, the code that makes it work, and a photo that tells a story are all the same problem to me: paying attention to what actually matters, then cutting the rest. I work fast, ship real things, and use AI-assisted "vibe coding" workflows to get from idea to working prototype in days, not months.

Off-screen I'm usually at the gym or boxing, out walking the dog, losing slowly at chess, editing a video, or grinding Duolingo Spanish. Grew up in Switzerland, Italian roots — I like things precise and I like them to look good.
