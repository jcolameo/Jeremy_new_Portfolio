My first real homepage

Started on WordPress in July 2015, way too limited creatively for what I wanted. So in winter 2018 I rebuilt it myself with HTML, CSS and JavaScript — my first real hand-coded homepage, and the thing that got me hooked on web development in the first place.

It's plain, a little dated, and very much a snapshot of where I was at the time — which is exactly why it's worth keeping around, even just as an archive.
